Daily life

  1. Start with a sentence

    오늘은 조금 바빴어요.

    I was a little busy today.

  2. Marc’s follow-up

    무엇 때문에 바빴어요?

    What kept you busy?

  3. Build on your answer

    할 일이 많았어요. 그래도 저녁에 산책해서 기분이 좋아졌어요.

    I had a lot to do. But I felt better after taking a walk in the evening.

Games

  1. Start with a sentence

    저는 게임을 좋아해요.

    I like games.

  2. Marc’s follow-up

    어떤 게임을 좋아하고, 왜 좋아해요?

    What kinds of games do you like, and why?

  3. Build on your answer

    저는 탐험하는 게임을 좋아해요. 새로운 장소를 발견하는 게 재미있어요.

    I like exploration games. Discovering new places is fun.

Ideas & technology

  1. Start with a sentence

    지금 작은 게임을 만들고 있어요.

    I’m making a small game now.

  2. Marc’s follow-up

    어떤 게임이고, 어떤 부분을 만들고 있어요?

    What kind of game is it, and which part are you working on?

  3. Build on your answer

    퍼즐 게임이에요. 플레이어가 쉽게 배울 수 있도록 첫 번째 스테이지를 만들고 있어요.

    It’s a puzzle game. I’m building the first stage so players can learn easily.

Meet Marc

Hi! I'm Marc Choi, a native Korean speaker with a background in game design and development. I studied Computer and Information Engineering at Inha University and have worked on game localization for overseas markets.

I have designed tutorials that introduce new players to games step by step and organized a university game development group. I would like to bring that same clear, practical approach to Korean conversation practice.

More about my approach

We can talk about daily life, hobbies, travel, work, or games you enjoy. If you are interested in games or technology, we can also practice explaining your ideas and projects in Korean.

I will adjust the pace to your level, help you find more natural expressions, and give you time to try again. At the end of each lesson, I will share a short review of useful expressions and sentences to practice. I can also offer simple explanations in English when needed.

These lessons are a good fit if you can read Hangul and want to turn short sentences into everyday conversations. Mistakes are welcome. Let's start with something you enjoy talking about!

Hi! I'm Marc Choi. I'm a native Korean speaker with a background in game design and development. Let's practice Korean through everyday conversations and topics you enjoy.

안녕하세요! 한국어로 편하게 대화하고 싶은 여러분, 반갑습니다. 저는 마크입니다.

저는 게임을 기획하고 개발해 왔고, 처음 게임을 접하는 사람에게 내용을 차근차근 알려 주는 튜토리얼도 기획했습니다. 이런 경험을 한국어 대화 연습에도 살리고 싶습니다.

제 수업에서는 일상생활이나 취미, 여행처럼 여러분에게 익숙한 주제로 이야기합니다. 게임이나 IT를 좋아한다면, 좋아하는 게임이나 캐릭터에 관해서도 함께 대화할 수 있습니다.

말하다가 단어가 생각나지 않거나 문장이 잘 나오지 않아도 괜찮습니다. 충분히 생각하고 말할 시간을 드리고, 표현이 어색하면 더 자연스러운 문장으로 함께 바꾸어 보겠습니다.

예를 들어, “저는 게임을 좋아해요”라는 한 문장에서 시작해, 어떤 게임을 좋아하는지, 왜 좋아하는지까지 차근차근 이야기해 보는 거예요.

한글을 읽을 수 있고, 짧은 문장을 실제 대화로 이어 가고 싶은 분을 환영합니다. 필요하면 영어로 간단히 설명하고, 수업 후에는 복습할 표현도 정리해 드리겠습니다.

여러분이 좋아하는 이야기부터 시작해 볼까요? 수업에서 만나 뵙겠습니다!

Questions before we begin

Do I need to know Hangul?

These conversation lessons are a good fit if you can read Hangul and make short sentences. We can use those sentences to build everyday conversations.

Can you explain things in English?

Yes. I can offer simple explanations in English when needed, while keeping Korean conversation at the center of the lesson.

What if I make mistakes?

Mistakes are welcome. You will have time to think, try a sentence, and say it again with a more natural expression.

Do we only talk about games?

No. We can talk about daily life, hobbies, travel, work, or other topics you enjoy. Games and technology are optional interests we can explore together.

Will I receive something to review?

At the end of each lesson, I will share a short review of useful expressions and sentences to practice.
